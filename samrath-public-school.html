About the School

A school built on academics,
discipline and support

Samrath Public School has been teaching students in Jaipur since 1998. We follow the CBSE curriculum from Nursery through Grade XII, with class sizes kept small enough for teachers to know each student individually.

Our teachers focus on building strong academic foundations while also encouraging discipline, teamwork and confidence. Alongside regular classes, students take part in sports, arts and a range of clubs that run through the school year.

Principal's Message

A note to parents and students

Every child who comes to Samrath is different, and our job is to help each one find their own strengths — in the classroom and outside it. We ask our teachers to notice the small things: who needs more time with a concept, who's ready to be pushed further, who's struggling with something they haven't said out loud. We believe good schooling is built on this kind of attention, not just good results. I welcome you to visit our campus and see this for yourself.

Mrs. Kavita Rathore
Principal, Samrath Public School
